# Product Context: ModularStep B2B Portal

## Why the Product Exists
Industrial safety managers and logistics coordinators need pre-engineered access stairs and ladders that meet strict regulatory codes (OSHA, EN 131) without the complexity of custom field fabrication. ModularStep provides them with an interactive 3D builder to configure their stairs in real-time, get immediate dimensions and weight estimates, and request quotes instantly.

## Target User Workflows
1. **Explore:** User lands on homepage, reviews product sectors (Industrial, Scaffolding, Warehouse, Aviation).
2. **Configure:** User alters step count, handrails, and platform sizes in the 3D visualizer, or selects a saved database preset.
3. **Verify:** User verifies compliance indicators and estimated weight/footprint.
4. **Quote:** User clicks "Request Quote", pre-filling their custom specifications in the contact form.
5. **Manage (Admin):** Admins log in to the backend dashboard to register new standard configurations, instantly updating the homepage configurator options.
